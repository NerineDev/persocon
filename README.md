<h1 align="center">Persocon</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Client Operations Platform for freelancers and small service businesses.</strong></p>

Persocon brings the operational side of a service business into one connected platform: clients, appointments, services, communication, payments, billing and day-to-day business management.

The project began as an appointment-management platform for my **Proyecto Integrado (PI)** in the *Desarrollo de Aplicaciones Web (DAW)* programme. The academic MVP remains the foundation, but Persocon is now evolving beyond its original scope into a broader product for independent professionals and small service businesses.

> 📄 **Spanish version:** [`README.es.md`](README.es.md)

---

## Product direction

Persocon is designed around the full lifecycle of a service rather than treating booking as the whole product:

**discovery → booking → payment → follow-up → appointment/service → billing → ongoing client relationship**

Its goal is to reduce the administrative work surrounding service delivery while keeping the experience understandable for small businesses that do not need the complexity of a full ERP.

Core product areas include:

- **Clients** — client records and connected operational history.
- **Calendar & appointments** — availability, bookings and professional time management.
- **Services** — management and public presentation of professional services.
- **Payments & billing** — payment status, invoices and connected financial workflows.
- **Messaging** — communication between clients and professionals with relevant context.
- **Business operations** — dashboards, reminders, workflow automation and day-to-day administrative support.
- **Discovery** — helping clients find appropriate professionals and services.

Persocon is not intended to become a general-purpose ERP. The focus is the operational layer around client-based service businesses.

## From academic project to product

The original DAW Proyecto Integrado established a functional MVP with authentication and roles, professional profiles, services, availability, public discovery, bookings, payments, billing, messaging, support and a SPA frontend.

The current product phase preserves that baseline while evolving the information architecture, user experience and commercial feature set toward a lightweight ClientOps platform.

The application source code is maintained separately in a **private repository**. This public repository documents the project's academic history and selected parts of its ongoing product evolution.

## Current status

Persocon is in an active **post-PI product evolution** phase. Current work is focused on rebranding, product architecture, UX/UI modernization and preparing the existing MVP for a broader ClientOps direction.

The public documentation is therefore a mixture of:

- historical academic material,
- the functional MVP baseline,
- and newer product-planning documents describing Persocon's continued development.

These are living documents and may change as the product evolves.
